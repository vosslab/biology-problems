# Advanced restriction digest problem ideas

These are separate future problem families. They are not proposed changes to
[linear_digest.py](linear_digest.py), which remains a linear-DNA, multiple-answer
question asking students to select observed band lengths. Current cut-placement
rules are in [CUT_PLACEMENT.md](CUT_PLACEMENT.md).

## Diagnostic digest design

Give students two plausible restriction maps for the same construct. They choose
the enzyme or double digest that would produce distinguishable gel patterns.
This assesses experimental design as well as fragment calculation.

## Missing-site inference

Show a partial restriction map and a complete observed digest pattern. Students
identify the only missing site location or enzyme identity that satisfies every
fragment length.

## Restriction fragment length polymorphism

Present wild-type and mutant alleles where a mutation creates or removes one
restriction site. Students predict the gel band lengths for each allele or a
heterozygous sample.

## Fragment count versus band count

Use a digest that produces two equal-sized fragments. Students distinguish the
number of DNA fragments from the smaller number of visible gel bands caused by
co-migration.

## Map validation from digest evidence

Give a proposed restriction map plus one or more digest lanes. Students select
the map revision that explains all observed bands or identify the result that
contradicts the proposed map.
