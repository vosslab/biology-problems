# Pedigree visual-review rubric

This fixed rubric is the source of the visual ratings summarized in
[PEDIGREE_EVIDENCE.md](PEDIGREE_EVIDENCE.md). It rates rendered topology for upper-level biology
teaching. The production biology, answerability, teaching-profile, difficulty, and layout checks
remain separate authorities in [PEDIGREE_BIOLOGY.md](PEDIGREE_BIOLOGY.md),
[PEDIGREE_DIFFICULTY.md](PEDIGREE_DIFFICULTY.md), and [PEDIGREE_LAYOUT.md](PEDIGREE_LAYOUT.md).

## Fixed review instructions

The following wording and scale are preserved from the review packets:

> Judge the rendered topology as an upper-level biology teaching diagram. Biological validity
> and answerability are already gated and must not be rescored. Ignore affected proportions,
> mode, labels, and hidden metadata. Do not reward size alone.
>
> - Structural interest (1-5): varied relationship paths, branching or joining families rather
>   than a repetitive single spine; useful visual exploration.
> - Compositional balance (1-5): branches distributed coherently without one crowded or empty side;
>   balanced need not mean perfectly symmetrical.
> - Readability (1-5): relationships easy to trace at the supplied size; avoid clutter and excessive
>   connector spans.
> - Overall usefulness (1-5): interesting, clear diagram worth selecting over peers of similar
>   workload. This is a holistic judgment, not an arithmetic average of the other ratings.
>
> Report observed facts separately from judgment. Supply all four ratings per numbered case,
> brief reasons for the strongest/weakest examples, concrete disagreements, and limitations.
> Review images before reading measurements or proposed formulas. The rubric stays fixed between
> rounds. Independent reviewers do not see each other's judgments.

## Interpretation limits

The rubric specifies four integer scales from 1 to 5 but defines no verbal anchor for each score
within that range. Integer ratings are coarse, ordinal judgments, not a calibrated interval scale.
Overall usefulness is explicitly holistic, so it must not be reconstructed as the mean of the other
three scores.

Ratings describe reviewers' judgments of images at the supplied size. They do not establish student
learning, instructor preference, biological probability, or the quality of unreviewed output.
Sample sizes, review design, disagreements, and study-specific qualifications belong with each
result in [PEDIGREE_EVIDENCE.md](PEDIGREE_EVIDENCE.md).

The preserved original is `../pedigree-aesthetics/RUBRIC.md`; each blinded review round used a copy
of that text. The fixed wording is copied here so this repository retains the evidence contract
without relying on the sibling research checkout.
